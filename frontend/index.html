About Ordia

Make Your Learning Journey More Organised

Ordia is a learning management platform designed to help people organise their learning journey in one place. Whether you are learning a new skill, exploring a different subject, or working towards a personal goal, Ordia helps you bring structure to the process.

With personalised learning plans, progress tracking, a community for discovering shared learning plans, a to-do list, and a focus timer, Ordia aims to make independent learning more organised, manageable, and consistent.

Our goal is simple: to help learners move beyond collecting resources and start making meaningful progress towards what they want to learn.

Plan your learning. Stay focused. Track your progress. Keep growing.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Ordia. We value your privacy and are committed to being transparent about how your information is collected, used, and protected when you use our platform.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Ordia may collect, how it may be used, and the choices available to you when using our services.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, Ordia may collect the following types of information:

Account Information: Information such as your name, email address, username, and other details you provide when creating or managing your account.

Learning Information: Learning plans, activities, to-do lists, progress records, and other content you create or save within Ordia.

Community Content: Learning plans and profile information you choose to share through the Community feature.

Usage and Technical Information: Information such as browser type, device details, technical logs, and website interactions may be collected if enabled by the services used to operate Ordia.

Payment Information: If you subscribe to Premium, payment-related information may be processed by the payment provider used by Ordia. The details depend on the payment system implemented.

3. How We Use Your Information

Information collected through Ordia may be used to:

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Save and display your learning plans and progress.
  • Provide the Dashboard, Community, To-Do List, and Focus Timer features.
  • Manage subscriptions and process payments, where applicable.
  • Maintain the security and reliability of the platform.
  • Respond to support requests.
  • Improve the user experience and platform functionality.

Ordia should only use personal information for purposes that are disclosed to users and permitted by applicable law.

Ordia allows users to explore learning plans shared through the Community feature.

Before publishing a learning plan or other content, review the information it contains. Content you choose to share may be visible to other users according to the visibility settings provided by Ordia.

Do not include sensitive personal information in content intended to be shared publicly.
